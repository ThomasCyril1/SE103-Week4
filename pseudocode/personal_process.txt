Problem:
Set up the church sound system before a service so that microphones and music can be heard clearly without unwanted noise or feedback.

Input:
Mixer, microphones, amplifier, speakers, cables, music source, and power supply.

Output:
A working sound system with microphones and music producing clear audio at appropriate volume levels.

Edge Case Handled:
If a microphone or cable does not work during testing, use another available microphone or cable before the service begins.

Pseudocode:
START
1. Check that the mixer, amplifier, speakers, microphones, cables, and music source are available.
2. Connect the microphones and music source to the mixer.
3. Connect the mixer to the amplifier and the amplifier to the speakers.
4. Turn on the equipment in the correct order.
5. Test each microphone and the music source.
6. IF a microphone or cable does not work THEN
      replace it with a working microphone or cable
      test it again
   ELSE
      continue with the sound check
7. Adjust the microphone, music, and master volume levels.
8. Check for unwanted noise or feedback and reduce the appropriate level if necessary.
9. Perform a final sound check before the service starts.
10. IF all sound sources work clearly THEN
       confirm that the system is ready
    ELSE
       identify and correct the remaining problem
END

Why this is clear enough to follow:
The steps are arranged in the order that the sound system should be prepared and tested. Each important piece of equipment is identified before the setup begins. The IF/ELSE decisions explain what to do when a microphone or cable does not work. The final sound check confirms that the system is ready before the service starts.
